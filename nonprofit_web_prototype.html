Our Work

A focused approach to closing gaps in specialty care.

In partnership with clinics and health workers across Jamaica, our work concentrates on four core areas. Each program is designed for measurable, durable impact in the communities we serve.

Clinical Care Missions

Volunteer physicians and clinicians provide gastroenterological screenings, consultations, and procedures at partner sites in Jamaica.

Medical Supply Procurement

Sourcing and shipping endoscopic equipment, diagnostic tools, medications, and supplies to clinics with limited infrastructure.

Clinical Training

Workshops and continuing education for local health workers in GI symptom recognition, triage, and patient referral.

Research & Advocacy

Documenting outcomes, sharing findings with stakeholders, and advocating for sustainable healthcare access in the Caribbean.

About the organization

Nonprofit is a 501(c)(3) charitable organization dedicated to improving access to gastroenterological care in underserved communities across Jamaica.

Our Mission

Reducing disparities in specialty care.

Nonprofit was established to address a measurable gap: in many Jamaican communities, conditions that are routinely managed in higher-resource settings go undiagnosed or untreated for years. Our model focuses on direct clinical service, supply infrastructure, and capacity building with local partners.

The organization grew from clinical observations made by gastroenterologists volunteering in the region. Today, we operate in partnership with three clinics, supported by a network of physicians, public health professionals, and student volunteers.

We are a small organization by design — keeping administrative costs minimal so that resources reach patients.

Our Principles

What guides our work.

01 Patient dignity

Every patient is entitled to respectful, evidence-based care regardless of geography or socioeconomic status.

02 Local partnership

We work alongside local clinicians and community health workers. Our role is to support and strengthen, not to replace.

03 Financial transparency

Annual financial reports and program outcomes are made available to the public. Donor trust is foundational to our work.

04 Evidence-based practice

Our clinical and educational programs follow established standards of care and are continuously evaluated for effectiveness.
